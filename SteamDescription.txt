[h1]Yoda's Tweaks and Fixes[/h1]
Application restart required after enabling this mod! 
Can be added/removed from ongoing saves.
SourceCode and Readme: [url]https://github.com/YodaDoge/ACSMods/[/url]
[h2]Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Spirit Animal now generates Leisure when performing any Playing actions or interactions with Disciples.
[*]Artifact Crafting message is not removed when the Artifact is hauled
[*]Outers search for training Dummies more diligently. 
[*]Tier 9+ clothing is ignored when mindful dressing is active.
[*]Ingame Copy function (see Hotkeys) now places the selected building and material in your hand. 
[*]ESC -> Difficulty -> Environment; now shows the correct map seed
[*]Universe Storage now shows items after opening
[*]Many search operations are no longer case-sensitive
[*]Mindful dressing now clothes naked disciples, even when their temperature range is met
[*]Disciples which cannot do adventures, due to pending Tribulation, are no longer selectable for adventures
[*]An alert is shown once shapeshifting tribulation has 100 seconds left
[/list]

[h2]Default Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Handworking Station Priority[/b]
Handworking is performed at the same priority as Stonecutting while respecting the priority setting on the workbench. 
[*][b]Smarter Dressers[/b] *
Mindful Dressers seek out additional items:
Outers equip Handkerchiefs, Bells and Talismans such as SpiritTravel, Cleansing, Agility...
Xiandao equip Scented Sachets and Dice
[*][b]Flag NPC with Manual[/b]
In the Search Panel, NPC's which still carry a Friendship manual have (秘籍) added to their name
[*][b]Smarter Bedroom Selection[/b]
Workers prefer beds in rooms with multiple beds and vice versa. Rooms with a single bed are named after their residents.
[*][b]Reactive Disciples[/b] *
Many resumable Actvities (leisure, cultivation, hauling etc.) are immediately interrupted when receiving new orders e.g. equip, eat, talk or miracles
[*][b]World Map[/b]
Disciples can be stopped from repeating their adventure via Ctrl+Click on the recall button. 
Disciple Names in the List are bigger sized
[*][b]UI Improvements[/b] 
- Many Windows have their Input fields focused initially
- Searching is now case-insensitive (Manual pavillion, Universe, Search Panel) 
- Manual Pavillion and Mini-Universe perform their search while typing 
- Items in Mini-Universe can be removed via shift+click (all), ctrl+click (10), alt+click (1)
- On Adventures with a single Disciple, interactions skip the character selection prompt
- Animal thoughts are now sorted by Type → Level → Name. Memorized shards use a bold font
- Spirit Fly toggles when a spirit contract is equipped, based on its speed compared to the disciple
- Dropdown on Disciples Panel to see/change their assigned Branch
- Shift+T with a disciple selected instructs their mentor to teach
[*][b]Rightclick Support[/b] *
On sect map use rightclick to
- Assign Beds/Cushions/Altars
- Talk to visitor
- Equip Items
[*][b]Meditation and Cultivation Tweaks[/b] [i]*[/i]
Meditation uses Spirit Root Sympathy. 
Meditation is cancelled upon dropping to 50 Mental State or less 
Inners on a balanced cultivation plan maintain at least 50 mental state with fun activities.
They cultivate until they reach a bottleneck; afterwards, they switch to practicing for XP and Qi-regen. 
Meditation is automatically used when available.
Standard Bottlenecks (no GC, Adventure, Peon or any costs) are automatically attempted, if chance is >50%
[b]Elysium Compatible[/b]: This logic is naturally bypassed if the Disciple is managed by Elysium
[/list]
*= Can be disabled in Mod settings

[h2]Optional Features[/h2]
Following Features must be enabled in the MLL menu (Esc => MLL)
[list]
[*][b]Auto Pause on Load[/b]
[*][b]Guard Disabled for Mentors[/b] 
[*][b]One Click Interrogate Extended[/b]
Almost identical to the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2856326732&searchtext=Interrogate]original[/url] Mod.
Interrogation includes all NPCs known to the player.
[*][b]Brighter Daylight[/b]
Days are brighter, creating a stronger contrast between day and night.
[*][b]Smelt after Transcribe[/b]
Manuals are consumed on sucessful transcription.
[*][b]Remove Map Fog[/b]
Disables Map fog on home and adventure Maps.
[*][b]Map Wide Branch Area[/b]
Branch area bonus is applied to their respective disciples at all time.
One such area per branch must exist on the map for this to work.
[*][b]Recruit without Priorities[/b]
Freshly accepted Disciples start with hard working enabled and no work priorities.
[*][b]Animal Autothink[/b]
You can define which thought shards an animal should use. 
It will automatically handle thinking, memorizing, studying and forming thoughts.
[*][b]Immortal Save/Load[/b]
[*][b]Show next policy event time[/b]
[/list]
